MyKIDS is a family app for Android. It helps you plan lessons, after-school activities and chores, agree on rewards and stay in touch with relatives. Children see their plans for the day and choose something to save for. Parents set the schedule and reward rules, then review completed tasks.

The idea is to make family agreements clear: what needs doing, when it should be done and what the reward will be. Grandparents and other relatives can join the family too, to chat, call and take part in shared activities.

## For everyday family life

- **A shared calendar.** Plan lessons, activities and recurring tasks, with reminders. Parents can add, correct and delete school grades manually.
- **Task results.** A child marks a task as done and can add a comment or photos. An adult reviews the result; completed tasks and decisions remain in the history.
- **Rewards and savings goals.** Children earn family coins for approved tasks and school grades according to rules set by their parents. They can choose a reward and save towards it. Parents set reward and penalty rules and confirm exchanges for money or rewards.
- **Family communication.** Family and private chats, voice messages, and audio and video calls keep relatives in touch.
- **A choice of look.** Five visual themes let the family choose the style they prefer.

For cash exchanges, the app uses **1 coin = 1 Russian ruble**. The parent provides the money or reward and confirms it in the app.

## From a task to a reward

For example, a parent asks the child to put their books away and sets a coin reward.

1. The task appears in the child's daily plan.
2. The child finishes it and submits the result, adding a comment or photo if needed.
3. An adult reviews it and confirms the reward. The coins go towards the child's chosen goal.

The time to do a task and the deadline to mark it as done can be set separately. A morning task can be marked later, while an adult still decides whether it was completed.

## Space adventures with Ray

Ray, called **Лучик** in the app, has his own stories, puzzles and games. In nine action missions, the child controls the robot, dodges drones, rescues other robots and uses dash, shield and upgrades chosen during the mission.

The action missions work offline and do not change the family coin balance. Parents can turn the adventure section on or off.

To sign in, you need an existing family account or an invitation from a parent in that family. Downloading the app does not create a family or give access to someone else's data.

Saved plans can be viewed offline. Sending messages, making calls, exchanging coins for rewards and updating family information require an internet connection.
